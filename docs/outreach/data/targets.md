## Honest note on the list

These are companies that *sell* AI into Annex III sectors. They are Tier 1
because they are the ones who can hand a deployer an oversight record as a
differentiator. But they are also the ones who might build it themselves, or
buy from a bigger vendor. The stronger long-term buyer is the deployer — the
HR team at a 300-person company using TestGorilla — and that person is much
harder to find from public sources.
