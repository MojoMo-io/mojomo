Two minds.

A private atelier for a creator and a co-creator. You converse; MOJOMO reflects; the ideas worth keeping are drawn out, named, and refined — one deliberate stage at a time — until they are ready.

Nothing here is measured in tokens. In this build, the only currency is the quality of the thought.
